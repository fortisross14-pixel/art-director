# Version 4 — Navigation Cleanup

This pass intentionally adds no new simulation systems.

## Changes
- Replaced the mixed Management submenu with a clear four-part structure: Museum, Collection, Institution, and World.
- Moved Expeditions out of Business and renamed the area Exploration.
- Separated Staff, Research, and Finance into direct destinations.
- Renamed the Codex to Collection Vault.
- Renamed the weekly screen to Calendar & Opportunities.
- Consolidated the duplicate exhibition views: clicking a room on the floor now opens the single exhibition editor used for theming, artwork placement, ratings, and room upgrades.
- Removed the secondary wing gallery view and its duplicate room cards.
- Added one simple museum expansion control below the floor.
- Preserved all existing mechanics and save data.
