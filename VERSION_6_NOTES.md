# Museum Wars — Director Systems v6

This release implements the connected management design rather than another isolated feature pass.

## Exhibition direction
- Every gallery now has a selectable curatorial thesis.
- Presentation packages can be matched to the room's artistic style.
- Art Quality is decomposed into collection strength, curatorial depth, and interpretation.
- Attractiveness now reflects thesis fit, décor fit, comfort, and presentation.
- Rooms show weekly visitor contribution, satisfaction, and detailed performance.
- Curators and researchers/educators can be assigned directly to rooms.

## Research and education
- Every art style now has a six-project knowledge tree.
- Projects progress weekly, cost money, and improve exhibition interpretation and curatorial depth.
- Cross-period dialogue is an advanced research outcome rather than an automatic cohesion loophole.
- Existing new-style institutional research is retained as the gateway to each tree.

## Audience and business
- Five visitor segments are calculated: tourists, enthusiasts, scholars, families, and members.
- Neighborhood identity now changes audience demand.
- The weekly director brief explains operating performance and identifies weak rooms.
- Memberships grow and decay dynamically according to museum appeal.
- Temporary exhibitions can be launched from eligible rooms with duration, marketing, and ticket surcharge decisions.
- Temporary exhibitions increase demand and revenue while active.

## World simulation
- Rival museum statistics now move with time rather than remaining completely static.
- Museum relationship state and outreach logic are available for future loan/collaboration events.

## Compatibility
- Existing saves are migrated with safe defaults for all new fields.
- Existing artwork, auction, exploration, gala, black-market, neighborhood, sponsor, multi-museum, and collection systems remain intact.
