# Version 16 — the revamp

A new UI from the ground up, following the revamp mock-ups.

## Navigation
- Four menus, each with sub-tabs:
  - **Week:** This Week, Crates, News.
  - **Museum:** Floor, Collection, Catalogue, Building.
  - **Acquire:** Calendar, Auctions, Expeditions, Artists.
  - **Office:** Plan, Money, Staff & research, Standing.
- The header shortcuts: cash opens Money, the initiative tokens open the Plan, and the museum name goes home.

## This Week
- Every opportunity for the week is a card with Go or Skip: sales, galas, the dealer, estates, digs that are back, crates and claims.
- A badge on the Week menu counts them.
- Ending the week with something unplayed asks first.

## Gallery screen
- A full-screen room with the following:
  - An editable name.
  - A wall of five frames, where you tap + to hang a work.
  - Visitors standing in the room.
  - Curator and educator slots.
  - Rating bars and thesis/decor/presentation chips, plus a back button.

## Acquire
- **Calendar:** the next 13 weeks.
- **Auctions:** each house shows when it sells next.
- **Expeditions:** Where → How big → Who leads → Send.

## The games
- **Gala:** a deckbuilder.
  - Play 5 conversation cards and discard up to 3. Wit doubles the next card. Each guest's hidden tastes are revealed as you play.
  - Your staff and background add cards to the deck.
  - Charm goals are 9, 13 and 17. A bot playing well wins about 90%, 70% and 43%.
- **Dig:** push your luck.
  - There are three trenches. A second card of the same hazard collapses a trench, so bank in time.
  - Every dig brings home one card, and each 3 fragments add another. That's about 1.5 cards for a Field dig and 4–5 for a Grand one.
- **Dealer:** flip 3 of the 6 evidence cards, then pay, counter or walk. Countering when you've seen warning signs usually works; countering on a bluff is risky.
- **Auction:** view the lot before bidding (1 initiative) to tighten the estimate. Bidders near their limit tremble.
