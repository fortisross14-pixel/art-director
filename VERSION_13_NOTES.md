# Museum Wars v1.3: Legacy (Milestone 6)

Careers now end, and what you built stays. Plain UI until the M7
revamp.

## Ending a career
- **Retire** at any time from the Calendar's season panel (it asks to
  confirm), or serve the full **35 years**, when the career ends
  automatically.
- The panel shows "Year N of 35".
- At the end you get a **career summary**:
  - Founder, district, museum type and funding model.
  - Final league standing.
  - Peak visitors per day and peak reputation.
  - Most valuable work and signature work.
  - Collection size and value.
  - Awards, discoveries, sets and best critic score.
  - A **legacy score**.
- The save slot is marked "retired".

## Hall of Fame (home screen)
- Every finished career is kept in the browser, across all three save
  slots, and survives deleting the slot.
- **All-time records:** greatest legacy, most visitors, highest
  reputation, longest career, most valuable masterpiece.
- **History table:** museum, founder, district and type, years, peak
  visitors, peak reputation, final standing, masterpiece, legacy score.

## Past museums in later careers
- A new career's world includes every museum from the Hall of Fame as a
  rival institution, at its final reputation, in the league where it
  finished. All of them appear in the World League.
- The league table in the season panel marks them "your past museum".

## Legacy score
- Base: peak reputation × 2 + collection value ÷ 5,000 + 50 per award +
  100 per set + 40 per discovery.
- Bonuses: +500 for finishing in the National League, +1,500 for the
  World League, and +1,000 more for a World top-10 finish.

## Scope change
Generated cities and career-length options were dropped at your
request. The current city stays, and careers are capped at 35 years.
