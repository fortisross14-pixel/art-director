# Art Director v1.5: Less text, more game

The four ways to win art, rebuilt as games.

## Auctions: a room full of bidders
- Before the hammer you only see the **estimate range** ("§2,700–4,100").
  The range is narrower in styles you know well. The **real appraised
  value stays hidden** until the lot is sold.
- **2–4 paddles in the room, each with a hidden limit:**
  - **Dealers** want a margin and drop out early, which gives you
    steals.
  - **Collectors** pay for love.
  - **Fixated collectors** (about 1 in 7) go far past the value.
  - **Cousins** collecting the style come in person.
  - About 1 in 4 lots gets a **telephone bidder** who rings in partway
    through.
- Bidders **hesitate** near their limit, so the count runs longer when
  they're close.
- **Jump bid:** three increments at once. It can spook timid bidders out
  of the room.
- **Paddles light up** when they bid and drop with a strike when they
  quit.
- **After the hammer** the appraisal is revealed with a deal rating:
  **Steal!** / Good buy / Fair price / Paid up / **Overpaid**. You get
  the same reveal when someone else wins: "they paid 140%. Let them
  have it."

## Dig board: a real deduction puzzle
- Every dug tile shows **gold = finds nearby** and **red = hazards
  nearby**, diagonals included.
- Empty ground **cascades open for free**, and the first dig is always
  safe.
- **One survey** per dig marks a 3×3 area without digging.
- **Gauges:** shovels (digs left), helmets (hazards you can survive),
  and a crate meter (★ relic = 1 card, ◈ fragments: 4 make a card).
- Dig, sparkle and screen-shake animations.

## Black market: Under the lamp
- The work sits on a 4×4 grid under a UV lamp. You get 5 scans, plus
  more with an authenticator curator.
- **Tells** come from the hidden verdict:
  - ✓ period-correct
  - ◐ restored
  - ✕ modern material
  - ⊛ registry stamp
  - ? wrong hand
- **Then choose:**
  - **Buy.**
  - **Haggle:** the evidence you found is leverage, but it uses up the
    dealer's patience.
  - **It's stolen!** Only offered once you've found a stamp. Right, and
    the price collapses; wrong, and the dealer leaves.
  - **Walk.**
- Buying reveals the verdict and the real worth.

## Gala: Charm the guest
- Pick a guest, then the work you'll ask for (an easy, real or big
  ask).
- Play topic cards from a hand of three: Prestige, Scholarship, Rarity,
  Fame, Money, The Public, Flattery, Legacy.
- **Each card reveals** whether the guest likes or dislikes that topic,
  shown as chips by their portrait, so you learn as you play.
- The mood bar must pass the gold line before the turns run out. The
  guest's face warms or stiffens with each card.
- The old dialogue text is gone.
