# Museum Wars — Director Revamp v3

This pass rebuilds the management layer around the museum floor rather than a row of disconnected tabs.

## New management shell
- Floor Plan is the default and primary game view.
- Navigation is reorganized into Museum, Director, and World groups.
- The top ribbon now surfaces cash, visitors, revenue, art quality, attractiveness, and world fame.
- Pending acquisition opportunities are visible from anywhere.
- City/neighborhoods, calendar/events, staff/business, research, rivals, and the collection database remain available from the director sidebar.

## Exhibition simulation
Each room now tracks three persistent presentation investments:
- Decoration
- Interpretation / learning content
- Visitor comfort

Each exhibition receives three live ratings:
- Art Quality: collection strength plus interpretation.
- Attractiveness: thematic cohesion, decoration, comfort, and density.
- Cohesion: links between artworks through artist, medium, era, and broad movement.

These ratings now affect visitor demand and therefore weekly revenue. Existing saves are compatible; old rooms begin with presentation level 0.

## Floor interaction
- Clicking a room opens a contextual director panel directly below the floor.
- The panel explains the ratings and lets the player buy room-specific improvements.
- The visual floor has been enlarged and restyled toward a colorful, chunky Kairosoft-era tycoon presentation.

## Preserved systems
The inherited-art opening, cousins, local and international museums, neighborhoods, multiple buildings, auctions, gala loans, expeditions, black market, sponsors, research, staff, rankings, and the full artwork database remain intact.
